# Sixteen pronunciation photo activities

Adds gardening, food, hotel and museum activities using four existing licensed local photos from the academy photography ledger. The original twelve activities remain.

Each new activity has three practice words, a sound focus, an original model sentence and Arabic meaning, an open question and Arabic meaning, two answer frames, two optional manual content checks and two optional manual sound checks. The existing private answer, second attempt, sentence plan, listening and recorder UI are reused. No automatic grading or pronunciation claims are added.

Checks: all sixteen data records, images and EN/AR/DE labels; full existing offline suite; TypeScript and Next export; all sixteen photos and second-attempt flows at 390px in English and Arabic. Recorder checks cover permission denial, not actual recording. TeacherDashboard is unchanged.
