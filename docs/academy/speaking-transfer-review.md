# Reflect and retry after speaking transfer

Transfer practice previously ended at recording. An optional closed self-review now asks whether the learner addressed the changed situation, reused a lesson phrase with a personal detail, and added a fitting reason/detail/follow-up. They can write one small target for another spoken attempt. This is explicitly self-review, not AI grading, and neither checkbox state nor target affects lesson completion or points. No network, persistent storage, automatic microphone access or recording change. Notes reset on task, close or lesson change. EN/AR/DE.
