# Contextual scenario teaching tips

Reviewed 25 November 2026. Scope: all 23 grammar tips in nine scenarios. Fourteen tips needed changes; nine stay unchanged. Dialogue, choices, feedback and scoring are outside this correction.

## Corrections

- Airport: greetings and suggested replies are options, not required openings.
- Restaurant and hotel: reservation/request examples are useful alternatives, not worldwide mandatory wording.
- Doctor and pharmacy: present perfect continuous is one choice, not the required structure for all symptoms. A continuing headache can use present perfect simple.
- Interview: STAR can help with past-experience questions. Describe actual actions accurately rather than inventing numbers or exaggerating.
- Coffee: directness depends on tone/context. To-go/takeaway varies by place. Extra-order and closing examples are optional, not universal formulas.

Arabic source and all 29 existing translation overlays retain localized guidance. English examples are preserved verbatim. Translation work is machine-assisted, with English roundtrip meaning checks across the affected entries; this is not a native-speaker editorial audit. Ambiguous Urdu, Malay, Polish, Turkish, Thai and Korean entries were revised and checked again. Romanian room/camera ambiguity and Hindi direct/obvious ambiguity were checked in their actual hotel context.
