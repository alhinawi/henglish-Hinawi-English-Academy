# Real-life transfer variations

After each of the nine real-life dialogues, learners can now open two additional original role-play variations in transfer practice. The 18 prompts ask them to handle a changed plan, unavailable item, misunderstanding or clarification request in their own English.

The optional disclosure stays closed until opened, remains behind scenario completion and the existing transfer-practice button, and resets on close or replay. Learners can invent details and use the existing private planner and recorder. The examples are explicitly language practice, not bookings, purchases or medical advice. Existing tasks, review criteria, scoring and vocabulary remain unchanged.

Verification covers exact coverage and distinct prompts, reviewed locale parity, full TypeScript/static export, and EN/AR 390px all nine scenarios: completion/open gates, closed default, exact English prompts and direction/language, private-plan preservation and close/replay resets. Existing transfer and review UI suites remain covered.
