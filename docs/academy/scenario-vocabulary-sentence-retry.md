# Real-life vocabulary sentence comparison

Every vocabulary term in all nine real-life scenarios can keep a private first sentence while the learner tries a second. Blank or whitespace-only first attempts cannot be kept. Starting again focuses the existing500characterEnglish field. Clearing the current sentence preserves the first. Each term is independent, and collapse, source-example hiding and meaning recall keep both. Replay or leaving the scenario resets both.

No new network, storage, grading or completion effects. Examples, meanings, dialogue scoring, replay and audio controls are unchanged. TeacherDashboard untouched.
